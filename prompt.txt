the next thing that research mode needs is the ability to solve to any intermediate position that is in cubeshape, not just solved. but to do that instead of completely overhauling the solver, we are gonna take a more brilliant approach and handle everything in the frontend.

lets say, we want a position "A2B1D3C45E6F7G8H" (given state) to solve to "A1B2C3D46F7E5G8H" (target state)....

step 1: compare the actual solved state (A1B2C3D45E6F7G8H) to the target state. and then for every position that is different from the solved state, we replace the same piece of the given state with solved state's one. for example: our target state differs from the solved state by 5 pieces. for 6 in the target state, solved state has 5,.... (the map is: 6 to 5, F to E, 7 to 6, E to F, 5 to 7). so, we replace these pieces in the given state like this: every 6 of the given state becomes 5, every F becomes E, 7 becomes 6, E becomes F, 5 becomes 7) (i said every because the solver accepts multiple instances of a piece). then give it to the solver and the solver output should be the solution from the given state to the target state. (trust)

step 2: if either given state or target state has placeholder pieces like UVWXYZ, then:
if the given state has placeholder: lets say our mapping says every 5 of the given state should be replaced with 7. but the given state doesn't even have a 5, rather has a bunch of placeholders (or 5 is non-existent due to some other piece being declared multiple times).... JUST IGNORE.
if the target state has placeholder: here things get a bit tricky.
you may already know, U correlates to {A,B,C,D}, V={E,F,G,H}, W={A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H}, X={1,2,3,4), Y={5,6,7,8}, Z={1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8}.
things are easy when we only have U,V,X,Y (no W or Z) in the target state or only W and Z (no U,V,W,X); or U, V and Z; or W and X and Y;  in the target state because their sets don't overlap.
in that case... first replace every well defined piece of the given state using the target state to solved state map. then give all the not defined pieces the placeholder based on their set. like if the target state was U1U2A3B4ZHZF7V8V, then after the normal replacement (from step 1), we are left with two Us, 2 Zs, 2Vs. give the remaining piece of the given state that are from U's set U, that are from Z's set Z and that are from V's V. so the given state from before ("A2B1D3C45E6F7G8H") turns into this: "U2U1B3A4ZVZF7V8E".

now if overlapping sets appear, then we need to account for all the possibilities that they can be in. like if the Us and Zs can be permutated in 3 different ways, solve all 3 and then keep the best one. like this: lets say the target state is U1U2W3D45W6E7G8H, then, try all the combination of replacing A,B,C with U,U,W; U,W,U; W,U,U; and then keep the solution that gives the shortest slice/move/angle count (depending on metric)
